# Independent evidence and experiment audit

Checked **6 November 2026, Europe/Moscow**. Canonical English; Russian translation (`evidence_ru.md`). This is a bounded desk audit, not a product test, market validation or permission to execute a study.

## Verdict

The six studies support **a hypothesis worth cheaply falsifying**, not an established maintainer beachhead, competitive advantage or business. Their important factual distinctions survive the spot-checks below. Published 1.4.1, native alternatives, corrected reports, missing human observations and maintenance uncertainty are represented honestly. There is no source basis for a platform expansion or a revenue forecast.

The initial synthesis overinvested in comparative-study machinery: three synthetic pairs before real-project research risk repeating technical acceptance rather than learning whether anyone has a valuable problem. The strategic challenger and this audit recommend one decision-bearing whole-workflow repair comparison plus a sufficient/no-change guard. Broader MCP, team and channel tests should remain optional branches. The maintainer choice partly reflects low investigation cost and fit to current functionality; it is not evidence that this is the best market. A maintained utility and a native-first repair reference should be peer delivery options, rather than merely consolation outcomes.

These are research-integration corrections for the root author, not runtime defects or release blockers. The [synthesis](README.md) owns the final ranked decision and experiment sequence; lane protocols are alternatives, not instructions to run them all.

**Integration recheck:** the revised EN/RU synthesis incorporates the peer delivery hypotheses and segment caveat, replaces the mandatory three-pair design with one repair plus a no-change guard, removes the universal percentage gate, and retains whole-workflow costs and all-start/eligible-repair denominators. Diagnostic success is separated from useful completion. No material audit finding remains open within the reviewed desk-research scope. Future execution still needs a concrete finite protocol; this closure accepts the research, not its unperformed experiments.

The final focused recheck of steps 2–3 also confirms that the sufficient-setup guard must complete a useful task; attempt/time/spend caps are finite and predeclared with no automatic retries; total active work, elapsed time and model cost remain separate. A removed intervention cannot outweigh added installation/review effort: the gate requires lower total active operator work or a specific tradeoff accepted before the run, without worse task quality. The conditional real-user round uses the same criteria. EN/RU agree on these points; no material issue was found.

## Experiment corrections and measurement contract

1. **Ask one decision-bearing question.** Compare current Tibicen's complete repair route with the person's competent current/native method, including ordinary edits and official guidance. Start with one actual setup change and next task, on equivalent saved starting states. If no person/project is available, a synthetic rehearsal may test protocol feasibility only; it cannot supply demand. Use a sufficient/no-change guard. Add an MCP case only if the chosen task needs it. Do not require another grader, evidence store or multi-harness matrix to reach this decision.
2. **Count the whole cost.** Include discovery/install, configuration/loading, proposal review, normal approvals, required manual work, correction, recovery and the useful follow-up task. Keep active operator time, elapsed time, assistance and model/cash cost separate; unknown cost is not zero. Match scope, permissions, versions, task acceptance and starting states. Disclose learning/order effects when the same operator sees both arms; changing order alone cannot eliminate them.
3. **Keep denominators and outcome types visible.** Retain every observed start and failed/incomplete/unknown outcome. Show useful completion among all starts, repair success among eligible repair starts, and justified no-change decisions separately. No-change may save work, but is neither a repair nor proof of task benefit. An installed folder, clean structural report or connected server is not a completed useful task. Init/adopt and assisted/unaided observations stay separate where both occur; continuation is not a new start.
4. **Use thresholds as choices, not facts.** The lanes' 20–25% time gates, 4/6 or 3/4 outcomes, channel counts and time windows are proposed small-study attention limits. They are not validated benchmarks, power calculations, release requirements or forecasts. Do not combine or cherry-pick them after results. Freeze the selected question, budget, success/preservation rules and stop rule before a future run; allow equal native performance to reject expansion.
5. **Measure reach and return only where observable.** Controlled exposures do not prove organic discovery. Report exposed people, starts and useful completions with each denominator. For return, distinguish eligible events, no event, prompted use, unsolicited choice and unknown/mature follow-up; do not call absent opportunity churn. Pricing interest is commercial intent, not payment or profitable fulfillment.

The synthesis should carry this contract without turning it into a new implementation backlog. Its later real-user round is conditional, not a promised cohort. [PILOT](../../PILOT.md) explicitly says the owner is unavailable for assessment/recruitment and human research is voluntary postpublication work. Missing people mean unknown human outcomes; they do not reopen a technical release gate. No coordinator, maintenance hours, commercial intent, payout eligibility or support budget has been established.

## What can be concluded now

Ranked evidence decisions: retain truthful 1.4.1 claims; test one cheap incremental-value hypothesis; prefer the native route/reference when it suffices; expand distribution, team features or commercial commitments only after the corresponding residual problem is observed. The strongest countercase remains that a short file and native audit complete the same useful task sooner, making Tibicen's installation and review net overhead.

If that countercase wins, narrowing delivery is a useful result. If a bounded comparison cannot be observed, keep comparative benefit unknown rather than funding a larger evaluation system. Actual demand, frequency of valuable repair events, unaided completion, preservation advantage in real use, owner capacity and paid fulfillment economics remain open.

Validation here is documentation reading, source spot-checking, paired-claim review and local link/whitespace checks only. No full runtime tests, live/model evaluations, recruitment, outreach, authentication changes, paid APIs, implementation, commits or publication were performed.
