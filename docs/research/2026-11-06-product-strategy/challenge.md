# Independent challenge: repair is a cheap hypothesis, not a chosen market

Reviewed **6 November 2026, Europe/Moscow**, against the draft synthesis and all six completed source lanes. English is canonical; Russian translation (`challenge_ru.md`). This is an independent strategic review, not another demand study or permission to execute experiments.

## Initial verdict

**Revise the synthesis before treating repair/migration as the next growth strategy.** The strongest conclusion is to hold scope steady while testing whether any additional Tibicen workflow earns its cost. Existing-project repair is economical to investigate because 1.4.1 already implements much of it. The research does not establish that maintainers are a beachhead, that repair is more urgent than initial setup, or that users want a separate workflow.

Promote a **native-first repair reference plus bounded maintenance of the current utility** to a peer option now. It should not appear only as consolation if a larger product fails. A short, version-bound explanation that helps someone finish with their existing agent can deliver the vision's outcome with less installation and approval work. Maintaining that reference still costs time; it is a delivery hypothesis, not a free escape from upkeep. Do not remove existing supported contracts on this review's authority.

The whole-workflow question remains correct: does the person reach the next useful task with less total work and preserved decisions? Setup completion, better-looking reports, more detected discrepancies and fresh-session loading are intermediate observations. None independently establishes that result. [Vision §§2–5, 14](../../PRODUCT_VISION.md), [pilot policy](../../PILOT.md).

## What is actually new

The source lanes improve the **substitution map** more than the demand evidence. Native initialization/audit, existing package lifecycles and experimental instruction-maintenance workflows materially weaken generic setup differentiation. Their documentation establishes competing capabilities, not their success rate or Tibicen's inferiority. The [competition lane](competition.md) supplies the broad inventory; this reviewer independently reopened three strong counter-sources listed below.

The [segments lane](segments.md) adds some previously unlisted original reports. Several concern loading scope or execution isolation that setup cannot necessarily fix. Skills #455, instruction sprawl and the small-team sharing discussion recur across earlier studies and current lanes. Fresh inspection improves their factual currency; it does not create additional affected users. The current [native observation](../../evals/results/2026-11-06-native-first-use/README.md) adds a failed useful-task attempt, with later phases unexecuted and the underlying operation uncorrelated. It supplies neither comparative benefit nor evidence that the product caused the failure.

The defensible delta is therefore **stronger reasons to demand a comparative result**, not stronger evidence that repair has become the winning market. This is useful strategic evidence even when it argues for doing less.

## Where consensus can mislead

1. **The segment follows the implementation.** Preserving local decisions, exact diffs and recovery are strengths of the present code. Selecting people who already need all three creates a low-cost test, but can select a tiny favorable niche. Experienced maintainers also possess the native/Git skills most likely to make Tibicen unnecessary. Qualify an independently existing problem before presenting Tibicen, and retain people who choose another remedy. [Segments](segments.md), [economics](economics.md).
2. **The lanes are correlated.** Shared instructions, product vision, baseline and earlier studies direct every researcher toward the same boundaries. Later lanes explicitly reuse the earlier lanes' competitor evidence. Six compatible recommendations are six analyses of overlapping material, not six independent confirmations of demand. Retain original incident identity and distinguish new users from rechecked URLs. [Source ledgers](segments.md#source-ledger-and-freshness-limits), [retention ledger](retention.md#source-ledger-and-unknowns).
3. **A repair can be real but economically irrelevant.** A stale command is easy to demonstrate, yet the owner's current agent may notice and correct it during the ordinary task. Test whether advance repair avoids actual work or failure, rather than merely moving an inevitable small edit into a longer preliminary process. The strongest control may be an ordinary task request with competent native help available, not a mandatory separate audit. [Distribution](distribution.md), [competition](competition.md).
4. **Many cautious experiments can still become bureaucracy.** The six lanes propose different sample counts, caps, percentages and staging. Those are attention limits, not validated success standards. Artifact preference, page comprehension and installer activation can all improve while next-task usefulness remains unchanged. Select one decision-bearing comparison before creating another protocol or measurement system. The existing pilot record can hold future human observations. [PILOT](../../PILOT.md).

These are limitations of the inference, not accusations of faulty source reading. The lanes generally preserve counterevidence and unknowns well.

The strongest case against the preferred repair route is that **Tibicen packages work the existing agent can already perform, and charges the user extra attention to obtain it**. Preservation rigor is necessary for a tool that edits configuration, but may be a cost of entering the category rather than a benefit users will seek. A maintained utility or reference can be the rational destination even without a scalable business. [Competition](competition.md), [trust](trust.md), [economics](economics.md).

## Smallest decisive falsification experiment

Replace the draft's immediate three-pair gate with **one whole-workflow repair pair and one sufficient-setup guard pair**. The MCP pair answers a different job and is optional later. This proposed comparison is not authorized or run here.

Choose one independently reported, minimized setup transition with a concrete next task and a valuable local decision to preserve. If no voluntary real case exists, a synthetic case can reject an implementation idea, but cannot establish urgency, choice or demand. Do not recruit by persuading someone that their adequate setup needs repair.

Before running, freeze the two initial copies, Tibicen release, host/model/settings, allowed paths and actions, task acceptance, preservation requirements and finite time/cost caps. Use the same caps in each arm; do not choose them after seeing results. The native arm can use its best available documented commands, ordinary edits and official guidance, including import if the job is migration. It may proceed directly to the task if that is the competent usual method. Do not reveal hidden defect locations to either arm. A supported and observable execution route is required; the closed native packet does not authorize retries.

Start at the same stated user goal. End only when the agreed ordinary task passes in a fresh session, or the cap is reached. Record setup/integration and task outcomes separately. Count learning and installation, approvals, reading proposals, correcting output, manual integration and recovery as operator effort. Record active minutes, elapsed time and observable model cost separately; unavailable cost is unknown. Use a short existing observation record rather than another evidence subsystem.

For the guard pair, give both arms an already sufficient version of the setup with the same intended behavior. It must complete the task without gratuitous repairs or loss of local decisions. Correctly saying no change is necessary does not establish value if the answer itself takes materially more work than the native route.

**Decision criteria, agreed before observation:**

- Both Tibicen paths preserve protected bytes and the named local decisions, report their actual limits truthfully and pass task acceptance. Setup must not modify the protected application; the separately approved task keeps its own scope.
- The repair path must remove a **named substantive correction or integration step that the native arm actually needed**, and use fewer total active operator minutes without worse task quality. An ordinary approval, extra report or a percentage on a tiny timing difference does not qualify. Publish the actual difference; choose any minimum material absolute saving for this case before execution.
- If native reaches the same useful outcome with equal or less effort, reject additional repair-workflow investment **for this job** and prefer the sufficient native/reference route. One repair does not reject broad setup or every other host. A preservation breach stops the comparison pending diagnosis, not all releases.
- If the native route is unavailable, both arms fail for an uncorrelated runtime reason, or the observation is censored, the strategic result is inconclusive. Do not award a Tibicen win or expand the evaluator indefinitely.

A pass permits one voluntary real-case follow-up when participants are available. It does not justify a market claim, an adapter program or the six lanes' cumulative experiment list. A small synthetic advantage must survive installation and normal review before it is meaningful. [Pilot measurement contract](../../PILOT.md#run-and-record).

## Three findings that would change the strategy

1. **Native sufficiency survives the whole workflow.** Equivalent accepted tasks and preserved decisions at equal/lower effort would move discretionary work toward native-first guidance and bounded utility maintenance. Repeated no-change overhead would strengthen that decision.
2. **Independent owners repeatedly choose and benefit from repair.** Naturally occurring cases show a substantive avoided step, lower total work and later unsolicited use at a real change event. That would justify investing in the existing repair path and only the support gaps those cases expose. An attractive proposal or assisted demo is insufficient.
3. **A team residual appears before a solo growth signal.** Independent teams repeatedly fail at the same exception-preserving transfer despite Git, native and APM/AgentRC routes, with a named owner and feasible support boundary. That could promote team interoperability ahead of broad solo acquisition. A single complex paid request instead supports investigating a bounded service, subject to operator availability.

These are growth and delivery decisions. They neither add human research to technical release gates nor erase existing engineering requirements. No owner questionnaire, recruitment assignment, live run, paid API or service obligation is created. The owner is currently unavailable for assessment/recruitment under [PILOT](../../PILOT.md).

## Required synthesis revisions

1. Label the maintainer segment as an existing-code-fit recruitment hypothesis; explicitly say there is no validated beachhead or evidence of repair's larger market.
2. Make native-first repair guidance / maintained utility a peer current choice and explain the benefit of avoiding installation when appropriate.
3. Describe the research delta as stronger substitution evidence plus a few newly listed reports, not independent demand corroboration from six roles. Add native import to the migration countercase.
4. Replace the default three-pair requirement with the two pairs above; leave other lane experiments as optional alternatives. Remove the blanket 20% time threshold as a universal go rule and restrict negative conclusions to the tested job.
5. Keep the distinction among engineering acceptance, strategic expansion and optional human/commercial research explicit. A failed runtime observation or absent participants is not a new release veto or evidence of no demand.

Root owns those edits. This review changes no source lane, roadmap or code.

## Focused synthesis recheck

The revised English and Russian synthesis gives the native-first reference peer status, calls the maintainer segment an unvalidated current-code fit, distinguishes overlapping source analysis from demand, includes native migration and replaces the immediate three-pair requirement with one whole-workflow repair plus a sufficient-setup guard. A second focused recheck confirms that it also requires lower total active operator work or a tradeoff accepted before execution, finite attempt/time/spend caps without automatic retries, separate active/elapsed/cost records and a passing useful task in the sufficient-setup guard. An avoided intervention cannot compensate for greater installation/review work. **All material strategic objections are addressed; no further synthesis revision is required by this review.** The detailed experiment above remains one proposed variant, not another mandatory protocol. No market or effectiveness conclusion follows from resolving this review.
