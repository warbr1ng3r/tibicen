# Tibicen on Agensi: distribution decision

**Research date: 6 November 2026, Europe/Moscow.** English is canonical; the adjacent Russian edition is README_ru.md. Completed: three background source lanes, root synthesis and a separate independent review investigate the owner's invitation from Joeri Brons. Independent review is recorded in [review](review.md). This is research and draft material, not a listing, product change or publication.

## Recommendation

**Do not upload the existing Tibicen ZIPs unchanged. First ask Agensi to classify an open-source installation assistant or a repository-linked editorial listing. If an acceptable route exists, test one free entry with a small maintenance budget. Do not build a premium edition or rewrite Tibicen merely to enter this marketplace.**

The previous chat explanation understated the delivery difference. Agensi is an MCP-mediated execution/content channel, rather than an ordinary downloadable ZIP mirror. Its [loading contract](https://www.agensi.io/docs/how-agents-load-skills) uses on-demand content and temporary standalone scripts. Current Tibicen instead requires an installed folder containing its CLI, imported modules and references. The mismatch is established by documentation and artifact inspection; no failed Agensi runtime execution is claimed. See [technical](technical.md).

A standalone, read-only diagnostic skill is the cleanest technical fit, but it would be a distinct, narrower offering whose usefulness is unproved. It is a fallback hypothesis, not the automatic next development priority. Keeping the current GitHub delivery and declining this channel is a reasonable outcome.

## Separate three decisions

1. **Product:** full Tibicen, installation assistant, read-only advice, or a new paid product.
2. **Package:** separate entries, one multi-skill pack, or a commercial bundle of existing listings.
3. **Business model:** free, one-time price, subscription, or a negotiated editorial arrangement.

Changing price or combining entries does not repair execution incompatibility. Likewise, technical compatibility does not establish visibility or willingness to pay. The [format guide](https://www.agensi.io/docs/what-you-can-sell) distinguishes a skill, a 2–50-skill pack and a discounted bundle of live paid listings; these are different operations.

## Credible approaches

Effort is relative judgment, not a delivery estimate. Every launch remains conditional on platform review and appropriate technical validation.

## Commercial interpretation

Free is the least costly learning model: no payout setup is needed according to the [creator FAQ](https://www.agensi.io/docs/creator-faq). It does not solve runtime or rights questions. A small one-time price could test purchase behavior later, but would also change visibility and buyer expectations; it would not isolate willingness to pay for the same audience.

The [pricing guide](https://www.agensi.io/docs/pricing-your-skill) permits one-time prices from $1 and subscriptions from $5/month, with payment restrictions at low prices. The [earnings guide](https://www.agensi.io/docs/earnings-and-payouts) and [affiliate guide](https://www.agensi.io/docs/affiliate-program) explain revenue and referrals. Platform analysis contains assumptions, reversals and payout caveats; its sample calculations are arithmetic, not a sales forecast. Skill revenue is tied to purchases/subscription payments, not automatically earned on every agent invocation; a free listing generates no sale revenue.

The referral incentive rewards bringing new people to Agensi. That is different from Agensi bringing new qualified users to Tibicen. Keep own referrals, platform-organic arrivals and invited tests separate when judging acquisition value.

Tibicen's [MIT licence](../../../LICENSE) permits sale and redistribution with its notice. Agensi's [Terms](https://www.agensi.io/terms) also impose restrictions on retaining/exporting supplied skill content. Ask how its delivery directive and buyer terms accommodate the licence and independent OSS installation; do not infer a compatible exception merely from its acceptance of open source. This research identifies a contract question, not a legal ruling. No licence change is recommended.

Payout eligibility is not established for this owner. Language and timezone do not identify residence, nationality, entity or banking eligibility. The payout lane records the provider-document inconsistency and exact questions. Do not assume that a stablecoin route bypasses account eligibility or compliance requirements.

## Visibility and truthful claims

The email's **65,000 monthly visitors** remains an unverified sender/platform claim. Other first-party pages use different metrics and populations; they cannot be combined into a traffic estimate. The [search documentation](https://www.agensi.io/docs/find-skills) discloses a preference for paid listings. A free listing can still be useful, but presence is not guaranteed qualified exposure.

The [creator dashboard documentation](https://www.agensi.io/docs/creator-dashboard) distinguishes views, agent uses and reported feedback. These are different from installation completion, an applied approved fix and a fresh useful task. Platform feedback is optional and model-reported; missing outcomes remain unknown. Do not invent a conversion rate if the exposure or attribution denominator is unavailable.

Use a bounded promise: help inspect project agent instructions, preserve useful decisions, propose exact changes through the full product, and report checks and limits. Do not claim proven time savings, automatic security, universal support for every advertised agent, unattended repair, or execution in a chat host with no repository tools.

The latest [repair comparison](../../evals/results/2026-11-06-repair-comparison/README.md) found the same repair and fresh-task result with direct Codex and Tibicen on one assisted fixture. It demonstrated no additional useful outcome or reduced human effort. This is counterevidence to a premium promise based on simple instruction repair, not proof that every Tibicen scenario has no value.

## Proposed next sequence

This is a decision sequence, not selected implementation tasks or permission to publish.

1. **Resolve classification first.** Use the unsent note below. Prefer an OSS/editorial route; otherwise clarify whether an installation assistant can install an independent pinned MIT release and whether its metadata may link the canonical source.
2. **Choose one honest product promise.** If only text-delivered skills are accepted, do not represent current full Tibicen as compatible. Consider the advisor only if it has standalone value; otherwise defer.
3. **Prepare a concrete candidate only after choosing the route.** Define exact files/version/rights, listing claims, host prerequisites and approval boundaries. A substantial wrapper or runtime adaptation needs scoped OpenSpec work. Reuse release integrity and recovery practices rather than manually patching generated metadata.
4. **Validate the actual delivery.** Inspect returned text/files and directives; test on a supported coding host with repository/terminal capability. Check declared read-only behavior, explicit install/change approval, correct source pin, missing-tool handling, preservation and a fresh useful task. Any live observations need their own finite protocol and budget. A local rehearsal is not an Agensi test. Agensi says serving is available only after going live; negotiate a test arrangement or document this limitation instead of inventing a private staging environment.
5. **If admitted, launch one free entry as a bounded channel test.** Use the distribution lane’s single proposed attention cap: four hours total for setup, clarification and QA, then at most 30 minutes/week for six weeks, including support and updates. These are stop limits, not estimates or an authorized schedule. If implementation would exceed the cap, defer rather than absorb an open-ended port.
6. **Review evidence before widening.** The distribution lane supplies a fuller pilot. Separate platform-organic acquisition from own promotion and tests. At day 30, fewer than 30 additional views is a low/unknown-reach result: end, or use one already-negotiated placement through day 45 within the same cap. At day 45, continue only with at least three independently initiated relevant task starts, one separately verified useful end-to-end session and maintenance inside the cap. These are screening rules, not proof of demand or superiority. If attributable exposure or outcomes are unavailable, retain the unknowns; do not infer product rejection or add a paid tier to disguise absent useful outcomes.

No participant pool is assumed, and this does not assign recruitment or a questionnaire to the owner. Human benefit and comparative improvement remain separate questions from whether the channel works at all.
