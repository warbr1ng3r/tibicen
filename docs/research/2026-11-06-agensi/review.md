## Assessment

No material blocking findings remain. The ranked recommendation is supported: reject unchanged full-runtime upload, clarify a repository-linked/editorial or independent-source installation route, and consider one bounded free experiment only if that route is admitted and validated. This is a research conclusion, not platform acceptance or publication approval.

The decisive technical premise survives challenge. [Agensi packaging](https://www.agensi.io/docs/packaging) describes isolated script execution; [loading](https://www.agensi.io/docs/how-agents-load-skills) adds retention constraints. The published CLI imports sibling modules and reads local metadata; model-context delivery does not supply that filesystem. A pack, lower price or single entry cannot repair this mismatch. A separately obtained GitHub release has different provenance from exporting marketplace responses, but [format rules](https://www.agensi.io/docs/what-you-can-sell) do not establish admission of that installation assistant. The reports preserve this uncertainty. Directory/editorial placement is likewise an unconfirmed negotiation option.

The alternative routes are meaningfully separated. A lite advisor would be new behavior, with no demonstrated reason to maintain it solely for marketplace admission. The [repair comparison](../../evals/results/2026-11-06-repair-comparison/README.md) supplies bounded counterevidence to simple-repair superiority, without proving that every Tibicen scenario lacks value. Pricing arithmetic is conditional rather than a forecast. Payout eligibility is unknown; the Agensi/Zoneless role discrepancy remains a question, not an allegation. The proposed day-30/day-45 thresholds and attention cap are author-selected screening rules, not statistical evidence or an active experiment. Unknown attribution and outcomes are retained.
