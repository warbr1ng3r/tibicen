# Agensi: commercial terms and listing choices

Research date: **6 November 2026**. English canonical report. Read-only public-source investigation: no registration, submission, outreach or payment test. Platform descriptions below are vendor statements, not independently observed service performance. This is a commercial decision analysis; unresolved legal interpretation stays a question for Agensi or counsel.

## Decision

The lowest commercial commitment is a **free, technically compatible offering**, with an explicit open-source licence clarification. The [technical study](technical.md) establishes that the unchanged release's installed runtime does not match Agensi's documented execution model. Do not upload it as a working full product. An existing-release listing or pack is conditional on a separately established compatible delivery route or explicit platform arrangement; packaging and price do not repair runtime incompatibility. Follow the [combined study's ranked recommendation](README.md#recommendation): first clarify an OSS/editorial entry or installation assistant, and consider a free experiment only if admitted and validated. Charging for MIT software is permitted by the licence; the difficult issue is whether delivery and buyer restrictions respect the existing licence and actual workflow.

The local [licence](../../../LICENSE) is MIT: redistribution and sales are allowed with its copyright and permission notice retained. [Product scope](../../PRODUCT_VISION.md) specifies two skills, `tibicen-init` and `tibicen-adopt`, and no persistent product runtime. [Release documentation](../../RELEASING.md#published-141--2026-11-06-moscow) identifies published 1.4.1, with source `319845f1f1597817807c81579b42250a1f189e7d` and distribution `52c0769574e90d7d1a0438996df9da40621b0107`. Listing a modified edition would require a separately reviewable, version-bound deliverable; this report does not create one.

## Available formats

Agensi documents individual skills and packs containing 2–50 skills; either can be free, a one-time purchase or a subscription. A pack is one ZIP and listing with multiple `SKILL.md` files. It does not sell locally installed plugins or host MCP servers. [What you can sell](https://www.agensi.io/docs/what-you-can-sell). The following are commercial options, not recommendations to upload unchanged release bytes. Neither a pack nor a bundle repairs the runtime mismatch.

| Approach for Tibicen | Why consider it | Main decision cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two free listings | Separate search intent and feedback for init/adopt | Two descriptions and update submissions; routing may split users |
| One free two-skill pack | One product, both workflows available | Must prove the agent picks the correct entry and resources |
| One focused free listing first | Smaller listing-maintenance pilot; either current entry retains both routes | A focused description does not reduce product behavior; a deliberately narrower product needs its own explicit scope |
| Existing MIT release sold once | Tests willingness to pay for convenient access | Explain the free upstream honestly; delivery/licence issue still applies |
| Separately valuable paid companion | Can address an evidenced task beyond the free kit | Requires new product work and a reason to pay; no evidence yet selects it |
| Subscription | Potential fit for a maintained knowledge service | Recurring result and support obligations exceed a simple ZIP mirror |
| Paid bundle | Discounted purchase of separate paid products | Only useful after standalone demand; not a free pack substitute |
| Affiliate only | Referrals without selling skills | Creates promotional work and dependence on attribution; not product validation |
| Defer or decline | Preserves the existing delivery path | Foregoes an unproven channel; sensible if required changes outweigh learning |

A bundle needs at least two live one-time paid listings. Its price is at least $5 and less than their total; subscription listings do not qualify. It is bought on the website, not by an agent; buyers receive the constituent skills. [Bundle documentation](https://www.agensi.io/docs/bundles).

## Setup, price and delivery commitments

Creator Mode, a profile and a submitted package are the documented path. Free listing is non-exclusive and does not require payout setup. The creator quickstart promises approval generally within hours, while the seller page gives 24–48 hours; treat timing as an estimate. [Creator quickstart](https://www.agensi.io/docs/creator-quickstart), [seller page](https://www.agensi.io/sell).

Paid listings start at $1; prices below $5 require buyer credit. Subscriptions start at $5/month, use cards and may offer yearly billing. An annual subscription commits availability for 12 months. Active subscriptions prevent switching pricing model. Changing free to paid makes previous free users buy to continue. [Pricing](https://www.agensi.io/docs/pricing-your-skill).

New submissions undergo automated checks, AI review and human approval. Network/local-data patterns and file writes can trigger scrutiny; warnings are not automatic rejection. Review is not compatibility proof. [Review rules](https://www.agensi.io/docs/review-and-approval).

Uploads are updated through a new ZIP. Buyers automatically receive the latest approved version, without another purchase; the previous live version remains during review. Agensi increments its own version and can automatically approve clean updates. Maintain an explicit map to the Tibicen release rather than assuming these version numbers coincide. [Updates](https://www.agensi.io/docs/updates).

## Licence, promotion and exit

The FAQ allows licensed open-source submissions and agent-created project output. This is useful affirmative evidence that Tibicen is not excluded merely because it generates files. [Creator FAQ](https://www.agensi.io/docs/creator-faq).

However, delivery documentation bars saving/exporting the skill and adds a purchase-specific notice to paid loads. Ownership remains with the creator, with a non-exclusive distribution licence to Agensi. An unpurchased listing can be deleted; removing one with buyers requires contacting support. [Protection and removal](https://www.agensi.io/docs/protect-your-work).

**Interpretation requiring confirmation:** a public MIT copy and Agensi's contractual restrictions are different legal layers. Do not assume either that charging violates MIT or that a platform notice automatically extinguishes MIT permissions. Ask for written confirmation that the MIT notice remains visible, applies to the distributed material and is not represented as proprietary content. Check rights to any third-party material separately. This is a condition for publication, not a finding that Agensi already breaches the licence.

[Terms](https://www.agensi.io/terms) are effective 2 March and updated **5 November 2026**. They ban outside paid upsells (§6.3a), permit documentation/source links, provide self-billing with creator tax responsibility (§7.5), permit future split changes after 30 days (§7.1), and allow sanctions-related USDC suspension (§7.4). Refunded/chargeback sales can reverse earnings (§7.1).

**Implication:** a free Agensi listing leading to a paid product on Agensi is a candidate; a free listing promoting an outside paid pack is outside the documented rules. Obtain the precise boundary for a general project website that also contains sponsorship or paid offers. Do not make a new annual subscription before the maintenance and shutdown plan is concrete. Deletion terms do not establish a guaranteed self-service exit after sales.

## Payouts and unresolved country eligibility

Agensi advertises a 70% creator share before buyer VAT; its 30% includes payment processing. Card revenue goes to Stripe on payment, credit revenue waits for $3.50. Stripe coverage is specified as EEA, UK, Switzerland, Gibraltar, US and Canada; other locations use Zoneless USDC on Solana. USDC becomes payable after 72 hours and is released at $3.50. Paid publication requires enabled payouts. [Earnings and payouts](https://www.agensi.io/docs/earnings-and-payouts).

The phrase “everywhere else” is not an eligibility decision for any particular creator. The owner's residence, citizenship, entity and payout account are unknown; timezone and Russian language establish none of them. **Russia-specific eligibility remains unanswered**, as do usable withdrawal routes, conversion costs and applicable reporting for the actual seller. Ask using real seller details before choosing a paid route; this study does not recommend bypasses.

There is a role-description inconsistency: Agensi describes Zoneless as its processor, while Zoneless's own terms describe noncustodial software with platform-operated transaction signing and compliance. These terms are dated 16 April 2026. [Zoneless terms](https://zoneless.com/terms). Ask who actually verifies sellers, owes payouts and controls funds, and which terms govern a creator. The difference alone proves neither fraud nor payout failure.

Zoneless explains that converting USDC to bank money is a separate step through an available exchange. Receiving a token is therefore not evidence that a seller can economically withdraw fiat. [Payout explanation](https://zoneless.com/blog/how-zoneless-payouts-work).

## Refund exposure and subscription promises

The guarantee covers failure to meet the listing promise, claimed within 30 days. One-time sales are judged as sold, with later dependency changes excluded when originally functional. Subscriptions must continue working; the policy describes monthly-payment or remaining-year refunds. Scope the listing to observed behavior and compatibility. [Guarantee](https://www.agensi.io/docs/money-back-guarantee).

Subscriptions include updates while active and access until the paid period ends after cancellation. Recurring billing should therefore buy a credible continuing service, not merely the expectation that an open repository may change. [Subscriptions](https://www.agensi.io/docs/subscriptions).

## Affiliate economics

Affiliate attribution requires a new account within 30 days of opening the first affiliate link, on the same browser/device. Own or existing accounts do not count. Commission covers eligible purchases in six months after sign-up, including subscription payments; it comes from Agensi's share. It is held for 30 days, then paid monthly at a $3.50 minimum. Own-skill commission stacks with creator revenue. [Affiliate documentation](https://www.agensi.io/docs/affiliate-program).

The advertised 90% is consequently conditional, not the ordinary creator split. Credit promotions can lower affiliate commission. [Affiliate terms, §7.6](https://www.agensi.io/terms). Joining and referring customers is a separate choice from publishing a free skill.

Illustrative calculations below are our arithmetic, not sales forecasts. Assume USD prices before buyer tax, full-price funded purchases, eligible referral where stated, no refund, no creator income tax, and no conversion/support cost:

| Example | Calculation | Creator/affiliate proceeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free use | No purchase | $0 |
| $10 ordinary sale | $10 × 70% | $7 |
| $10 own sale to an eligible referral | $7 + $10 × 20% | $9 |
| $10 other-creator sale to a referral | $10 × 20% | $2 |
| $20 list price with 20% deal | $16 × 70% | $11.20 |
| $9 monthly payment | $9 × 70% | $6.30 per retained payment |

If a pilot costs four hours valued internally at $30/hour, it needs **18 retained $10 ordinary sales** to exceed $120 in labour (18 × $7 = $126), before support or taxes. At five minutes of support per sale and the same hourly value, contribution is $4.50, requiring **27 retained sales**. Refunds reduce this; 100 sales with 10 full reversals yield $630 ordinary creator revenue. None of these inputs is measured for Tibicen.

Buyer credit currently starts at a $5 top-up, lasts 12 months and cannot pay subscriptions. This adds acquisition friction to a $1–$4 experiment; a low sticker price does not mean a new buyer only funds that amount. [Balance](https://www.agensi.io/docs/credits).

## What the pilot can actually measure

Creator Studio documents views, uses and sales, plus anonymous agent feedback with opt-outs. Uses exclude the creator and extra reference loads within the same use. Default thank-you/review requests are sent after purchase or first use unless disabled. Plan message settings before publishing. [Creator dashboard](https://www.agensi.io/docs/creator-dashboard).

Recommended interpretation: views are exposure, uses are attempts, reported agent success is a signal, and useful independent project outcomes require separate observation. A free pilot cannot establish willingness to pay. A paid pilot with mostly self-referred traffic cannot establish the marketplace's incremental discovery value.

## Questions that determine a go/no-go

1. Will Agensi preserve MIT notices and acknowledge their permissions for both free and paid Tibicen content, including its buyer-facing load notice?
2. Can the actual init/adopt workflow load all references and execute authorised local work without saving prohibited skill files? Which parts are permissible project output?
3. Which concrete seller country/entity/payment details are eligible, and who contracts, checks and pays through Zoneless? What costs or holds remain after the advertised split?
4. Can an existing listing be hidden from new acquisition while preserving buyers, and what happens to updates, pending balances and active annual commitments on exit?
5. How are Agensi's generated version, Tibicen's release SHA and rollback handled? Can a creator prevent an unsuitable update becoming the new default?
6. Is a source link to a general site with sponsorship or paid products allowed? Can Agensi distinguish an informational source link from a prohibited upsell in writing?
7. Can discovery be measured separately from creator referrals, with views → use → return-use data and an explicit observation period?

Publication is a later owner decision after those answers and technical verification. This research authorises no account or marketplace action.
