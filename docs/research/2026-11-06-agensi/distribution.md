# Agensi: audience, positioning and distribution approaches

Checked **6 November 2026, Europe/Moscow**, against published **Tibicen 1.4.1**. Research and dormant copy only; no account, listing, outreach, purchase, live evaluation or new telemetry. Commercial/legal and delivery details belong to the other research lanes.

## Recommendation

**Treat Agensi as a bounded additional discovery experiment, conditional on a truthful delivery and licence arrangement.** First ask whether an OSS directory/editorial entry can point to Tibicen's existing GitHub distribution. If that is unavailable, consider a free installation assistant that points to independently obtained MIT source, but only after Agensi expressly permits that arrangement and its licensing boundaries are clear. These are proposed arrangements, not features established by the seller invitation. Declining is preferable to maintaining a second product solely to qualify for the marketplace.

A complete free init/adopt listing would be attractive if it could preserve the existing product. It is not presently a demonstrated delivery route. A read-only advisor is a different product and earns a place only if it has useful standalone behavior. Do not strip Tibicen down to a generic checklist or one-line repair just to fit text delivery. The latest [repair comparison](../../evals/results/2026-11-06-repair-comparison/README.md) found that both direct Codex and Tibicen completed the synthetic case; Tibicen added no functional result there. The [product strategy](../2026-11-06-product-strategy/README.md) and [vision](../../PRODUCT_VISION.md) therefore remain constraints on marketing claims, not proof of demand.

## What discovery actually offers

Agensi documents category/keyword browsing, price filters and several sorts. Its **agent search favors paid skills when they are at least 80% as relevant as a free result**; website AI search places paid results first within relevance groups. Free is supported, but equal visibility is not promised. [Find skills](https://www.agensi.io/docs/find-skills).

The live [catalog](https://www.agensi.io/skills) exposes Agents & Orchestration, Prompt & Skill Engineering, Code Quality & Review and LLM Ops: plausible placement for Tibicen, not evidence of demand. There are over 18,000 listed items. The [homepage](https://www.agensi.io/) also has featured, recent and trending surfaces; its new-week section says one item per creator is shuffled. Exact ranking weights, feature-selection criteria and Tibicen impressions remain unknown. A feature is an opportunity to negotiate, not an entitlement.

The company describes a broad agent-user audience rather than only developers. That makes aggregate traffic a poor proxy for people who have a local repository and an immediate instruction/setup problem. [About](https://www.agensi.io/about). A chat client with marketplace MCP access does not thereby gain local filesystem, shell, repository or native-agent access. Market the required capabilities and supported delivery route, not a row of agent logos.

**The email's 65,000+ monthly visitors is unverified.** No public, independently auditable primary analytics supporting that figure was found in the inspected sources or exact-phrase searches. It could mean visitors, visits, sessions or pageviews over an unspecified period. Ask for dated, anonymized analytics and the share reaching relevant categories; do not derive a sales forecast from it. Agensi's [promotion article](https://www.agensi.io/learn/how-to-promote-skills-on-agensi) asserts stronger conversion and sales from promotion without a disclosed study in the inspected text. Those are vendor claims, not Tibicen evidence.

## Search engines and the GitHub relationship

Direct HTML checks found indexable content, titles, descriptions and self-referencing canonical links on the inspected listing pages. [robots.txt](https://www.agensi.io/robots.txt) permits ordinary crawling and identifies [sitemaps](https://www.agensi.io/sitemap.xml) for skills, creators and guides; search-result URLs have separate restrictions. Search returned several specific listing pages. This demonstrates discoverable public pages in this research search, **not Google ranking, organic traffic or indexation of a future Tibicen page**.

An Agensi canonical currently points to Agensi, not to the creator's GitHub. A copied README therefore does not automatically transfer search signals to Tibicen's site. Google explains that canonical declarations are signals and it can select another URL. [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls). Use a short original task-specific listing, label GitHub as the source and independent installation route, and keep detailed release/version documentation there. This avoids divergent promises; no duplicate-content penalty or ranking benefit is asserted. Whether creators can choose canonicals, outbound-link attributes or analytics export is unknown.

Current MIT terms are in [LICENSE](../../../LICENSE); retaining rights to public copies is not an assurance that marketplace restrictions are compatible.

## Small measurable experiment, if a route becomes viable

**Dormant proposal, not authorization or an experiment already running.** No owner recruitment or interviews are assigned: [pilot policy](../../PILOT.md) says no available cohort/coordinator is assumed. No paid models, ad spend or telemetry implementation is needed. Existing platform counts and voluntary inbound reports suffice, with unknowns preserved.

Author-selected resource cap: **four hours total setup/clarification/QA**, then **30 minutes per week for six weeks**, including listing administration and unsolicited support. Reserve at most one hour inside that latter allowance for a release update. If the route requires a maintained fork, a new feature or an unbounded compatibility exercise, stop this experiment and estimate a separate project. These limits are proposed attention budgets, not predictions that validation takes four hours.

1. **Before day 0:** confirm the specific licensing/delivery route, one exact version, honest capabilities, independent source link and removal/update procedure. Validate the concrete route under a separately agreed technical scope. Do not start a public timer while these are unknown. Choose one surface; do not split traffic among many variants.
2. **Days 0–30:** one listing or negotiated editorial placement. Record initial/final views, platform uses, source of unsolicited inquiries, real task starts and support minutes in one manual table. Keep marketplace-native arrivals separate from traffic the maintainer brings. Do not message users or trigger a promotion campaign under this research authorization.
3. **Day 30 reach check:** if the available counters show fewer than **30 additional views**, classify reach as insufficient/unknown and end, or allow one already-negotiated placement through day 45 within the same cap. This is a channel-management threshold, not a minimum sample for estimating demand. High views with zero starts mean revise the promise once or close the channel; unknown start attribution stays unknown.
4. **Day 45 decision:** continue the free route for one more bounded period only if there are **at least three independently initiated relevant task starts and one separately verified useful end-to-end session**, while maintenance stays inside the cap. A voluntary user statement is a reported outcome; an agent “worked” report alone is not verification. Obtain any necessary consent before inspecting traces; absence of traces leaves usefulness unknown. With only starts or favorable comments, preserve the signal but do not launch premium content. With low exposure, conclude “this channel did not produce enough evidence,” not “nobody wants Tibicen.” Any substantive preservation/licence defect pauses distribution until resolved.

These small thresholds screen whether to spend more attention. They cannot estimate conversion, general efficacy or revenue. Useful no-change/diagnostic outcomes are recorded separately, alongside all failures, assistance and unknowns; they do not silently count as successful repair-to-next-task sessions.

The [creator dashboard](https://www.agensi.io/docs/creator-dashboard) documents views, uses and agent-reported feedback. A use is a signed-in non-owner loading session, not an outcome; repeated loads can count, extra files in one use do not. Feedback is optional and agent-generated. Native analytical dimensions/export are unverified. The [affiliate dashboard](https://www.agensi.io/docs/affiliate-program) documents referral clicks/signups and commissions; those cannot reveal Tibicen activation. Avoid adding affiliate onboarding to the first free experiment: it tests another relationship and adds disclosure/payout work.

## Truthful English copy drafts

**Not publishable until the selected route is permitted and validated.** No screenshots, testimonials or outcome examples were fabricated. These drafts intentionally avoid “works everywhere,” guaranteed safety, measured time savings and unsupported superiority over a direct prompt.

### A. Negotiated OSS directory/editorial entry

**Title:** Tibicen — set up or repair coding-agent project instructions

**Short description:** Open-source skills for reviewing project agent infrastructure, proposing exact changes, and applying an approved setup or repair in your local coding agent.

**Long description:**

Tibicen helps with two jobs: set up agent infrastructure for a project with `tibicen-init`, or review and improve an existing setup with `tibicen-adopt`. It inspects the project and selected agents, preserves useful existing decisions, and presents a change plan before applying approved infrastructure changes. Existing application code is outside that repair scope.

The project remains free under the MIT licence on GitHub. Follow the official release and installation guide for the complete skills and their supporting files. This directory entry is a discovery link; it does not install Tibicen or demonstrate Agensi runtime compatibility.

Requirements and checks depend on the chosen agent's access to your project and tools. Tibicen reports unavailable checks and manual actions. Broad native compatibility, comparative time savings and human benefit have not been established. A direct agent request may be enough for a simple correction.

Source and independent installation: [Tibicen](https://github.com/warbr1ng3r/tibicen). Confirm the listing's exact release before publishing.

### B. Free installation assistant, only if explicitly allowed

**Title:** Tibicen setup assistant — install the free project-infrastructure skills

**Short description:** Check the requirements and guide an approved installation of the independent MIT-licensed Tibicen skills for your local coding agent.

**Long description:**

This assistant explains Tibicen's init and adopt routes, checks the requirements available to your agent, and guides installation from the project's official versioned source after your approval. The complete implementation is obtained independently from GitHub; this listing does not deliver or replace it. Local filesystem and command access are required for installation; a chat-only client can provide instructions and disclose the remaining manual work.

After installation, use `tibicen-init` for first setup or `tibicen-adopt` to review and improve existing agent infrastructure. Review and approve their proposed changes before application. The installed Tibicen skills are free and MIT licensed. Their own official documentation defines supported behavior and limits.

This is an installation guide, not an assurance that an agent will complete every setup or repair. It does not require payment for Tibicen, promise measured productivity gains or provide ongoing support. Publish only after the actual assistant exists, version handling and execution consent are reviewed, and the exact Agensi route is verified.

**FAQ for the selected draft:**

- **Is Tibicen free elsewhere?** Yes. The complete published skills are available from the linked GitHub project under MIT.
- **Will this change my application?** The full Tibicen workflow limits existing-project writes to approved agent infrastructure; review its proposal and current documentation. This directory/assistant entry is not itself a completed repair.
- **Can I use it in ordinary web chat?** Explanations may be useful there; installing and validating local project infrastructure needs the appropriate project and command capabilities.
- **Is it better than `/init` or a direct prompt?** Comparative benefit is unestablished. A simple synthetic repair produced the same functional result with direct Codex and Tibicen.
- **What if Agensi is unavailable?** The independent GitHub distribution remains a separate route; do not claim the Agensi-delivered assistant works offline.

Add `agent-setup` or `project-instructions` only if custom tags are accepted; do not mislabel a tool as an MCP server merely for search traffic.
