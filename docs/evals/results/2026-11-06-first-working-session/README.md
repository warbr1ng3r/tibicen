**Limits:** these are synthetic, owned fixtures and assisted internal subagents, sometimes with fixture-author or implementation-reading assistance. Explicit project-instruction paths were supplied. The three root-supplied simulated mechanical approvals followed independent exact-plan review; **they are not end-user consent**.
