## Policy follow-up — 3 November 2026

The adopted [release evidence policy](../../../../openspec/changes/separate-release-evidence-from-usefulness-pilot/proposal.md) separates technical release acceptance from human usefulness research. Technical acceptance requires candidate-bound observations and independent engineering review while retaining the full original MVP functionality, strict invariants, functional quality, positive additional-skill loading/use and optional OpenSpec init/adopt/use, declared coverage, exact-candidate delivery/update/recovery and maintenance readiness. Reusable evidence and insufficient or missing technical observations are tracked in the separate [engineering assessment](../../ENGINEERING_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [readiness ledger](../../V1_READINESS.md). This policy does not establish 1.0 readiness or close the remaining private conduct, independent advisory/checksum and source-review procedure gaps.

Human usefulness observations and human rubric calibration are separate voluntary postpublication work. Measured benefit and comparative effort savings remain unknown. The [usefulness protocol](../../../PILOT.md) retains the 50-session/8-week checkpoint, 30-day reuse definitions and an explicit insufficient-data decision. Engineering review does not create a human grade or complete an old human-calibrated study.

This dated addendum changes current scheduling, not the at-freeze outcomes above. Their original filenames, hashes, failures, source/payload bindings and limits remain unchanged; sealed archives and visible release-draft files were not rewritten. No fresh model call, runtime/build change, candidate-byte change, commit or publication was performed by this follow-up.
