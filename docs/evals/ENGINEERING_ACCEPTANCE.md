## Method and reviewer context

The ticket 138 audit worker inspected the original maps, hash receipts, selected captured responses and command/read/write records independently of their recorded executor contexts. Previous independent reviews cited below accepted artifact accuracy or code/mechanical scope; their `Ship` verdicts are not semantic or human grades. Independent integration review of this new assessment is a separate task in the [policy change](../../openspec/changes/separate-release-evidence-from-usefulness-pilot/tasks.md).

Dispositions apply to the stated claim: **reusable-within-scope** means the observed subcriterion has adequate evidence for its original inputs/bytes; **insufficient** means relevant evidence exists but does not establish the required claim; **missing** means the specified observation has not been found. A row can reuse a mechanism while its agent decision remains insufficient or missing. This is not a count of accepted end-to-end cases.

The audit checked existing evidence rather than rerunning it: 23 mapped inputs, 17 supplemental inputs, 43 runtime-source bindings, 31 fixture-construction inputs, the 67 ordered IDs, and 80 mapped passed names plus five earlier and 13 later regressions in the saved gate. Twenty of the 23 mapped inputs are byte-identical; the three acceptance guides gained only the dated policy banner. Removing that exact banner reproduces each frozen hash, with scenario text unchanged. The first strict hash-check failure is retained beside this explicit documentation-only impact analysis. No skipped test received credit. All 86 paths listed in the current distribution inventory match their recorded hashes; its 84 non-package payload files match the corresponding source copies. The private receipt records these paths/counts, rather than asserting a broader runtime observation. Fixture digests were not dynamically recomputed; unchanged construction inputs and the existing passed matrix-lock assertion provide that binding. The private audit index retains the per-case fixture hash, exact test name/source/log line and inspected artifact hashes; public reports below provide sanitized context.
