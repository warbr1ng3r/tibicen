# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence determines readiness
The product contract SHALL distinguish file presence and local checks from observed agent behavior and measured user benefit. Technical release acceptance SHALL require candidate-bound evidence and independent engineering review; public research and agent opinion SHALL NOT become human observations or measured benefit.

#### Scenario: An implementation has not been tested with real agents
- **WHEN** only documents or local checks exist
- **THEN** they are not presented as proof of instruction loading, a successful working task or public release readiness

#### Scenario: Research informs priorities without a product pilot
- **WHEN** public discussions or studies support a setup hypothesis but no Tibicen participant outcomes exist
- **THEN** the report cites the sources and states that Tibicen-specific benefit is unmeasured
- **AND** research does not close a required technical observation

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Technical release acceptance preserves full MVP obligations
Technical acceptance SHALL retain the full MVP features, L0 strict checks, L1 hard invariants and functional quality, positive skill/process loading and use, declared coverage, candidate-specific delivery/update/recovery and maintenance readiness. Missing mandatory evidence SHALL block technical acceptance; a successful repeat SHALL NOT cancel a candidate invariant violation.

#### Scenario: Human ratings are unavailable but technical evidence is complete
- **WHEN** a candidate meets the technical requirements and has independent engineering review, while human calibration and pilot observations are absent
- **THEN** technical readiness can be recorded without inventing human grades
- **AND** public readiness explicitly discloses unmeasured benefit and still requires maintenance, immutable delivery evidence and explicit publication authorization

#### Scenario: A candidate lacks positive skill or process evidence
- **WHEN** tests or negative-source refusals pass but mandatory positive loading/use of an additional skill or the first optional process adapter is unobserved
- **THEN** technical readiness remains open with the exact missing observation
- **AND** an optional adapter stays optional for each target project even though its supported integration needs release acceptance

#### Scenario: An independent engineering assessment lacks sufficient evidence
- **WHEN** the reviewer has only an executor's claim, an unbound artifact or a result from an unverified version
- **THEN** the affected criterion remains unverified rather than becoming accepted through review alone
- **AND** the assessment records the criterion, source/version binding, observations, reviewer context, conclusion and limits

### Requirement: Human usefulness research has an independent schedule
The initial voluntary usefulness pilot and human rubric calibration SHALL NOT be prerequisites for technical release acceptance. They SHALL remain separately reported postpublication work with voluntary participation, separate init/adopt measurements, privacy and baseline rules. The existing 50-session/8-week checkpoint and 30-day reuse definitions SHALL remain; insufficient data SHALL be disclosed without fabricated success or automatic delay.

#### Scenario: No participants are available after publication
- **WHEN** a checkpoint is reached with insufficient voluntary observations
- **THEN** the report states insufficient data and records the decision to recruit, extend or close the study
- **AND** the clock is not reset and the owner is not silently enrolled

### Requirement: Engineering acceptance does not rewrite calibrated experiments
Existing experiment records, evaluated outcomes, failures, missing human cross-checks and calibration requirements SHALL retain their meanings. A separate engineering release assessment SHALL identify its evidence level. A study claiming human-calibrated acceptance SHALL still meet the relevant calibration and cross-check protocol.

#### Scenario: An old series has missing human quality review
- **WHEN** its observable artifacts are inspected for an engineering release assessment
- **THEN** the original evaluated result remains unchanged and the new assessment links its own scope and evidence
- **AND** no model answer is stored as a human grade, no known failure is erased, and the old series is not relabeled as accepted human-calibrated evidence

#### Scenario: A new observation is required
- **WHEN** the evidence audit identifies a necessary fresh model run after the existing executor ceiling is exhausted
- **THEN** the missing check remains open until a separate finite execution plan and authorization exist
- **AND** adopting this policy does not itself grant model budget, account changes or publication permission
