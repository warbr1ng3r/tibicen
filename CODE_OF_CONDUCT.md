# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss code and decisions with concrete
examples; make room for different experience levels and constructive disagreement.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual remarks, publication of private
information and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable in project spaces,
issues, pull requests and project-related contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or exclude
a participant. They should explain decisions privately where practical and avoid
disclosing a reporter's identity or unrelated personal information. A maintainer
involved in a report should leave its handling to another responsible person.

## Reporting

The responsible maintainer and private conduct reporting contact are not yet
designated. They remain open prerequisites for the full MVP; the limited alpha does not
claim an operational conduct reporting route. Do not put sensitive reports in a public issue.
Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
